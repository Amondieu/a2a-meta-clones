How it works

Three steps. No platform migration.

Configure scope + S3 evidence URIs

Point the loop at your deployment: deployment_id, evaluation period, and the S3 URIs where your operational evidence already lives.

Monthly or on-demand run

Schedule the loop monthly or trigger it on demand before an audit. Each run evaluates all five obligations and writes the full artifact set.

Replay any audit_id

Any past run can be replayed byte-identically within the retention window. The replay command ships inside the evidence package itself.

What this is not

Read this before you buy

This loop does one thing: deterministic Article 26 deployer evidence. If you need any of the following, buy that instead.

Not a GRC platform

No dashboards of dashboards, no workflow engine, no policy library. It emits evidence artifacts; your GRC tooling can ingest them.

Not a policy generator

It will not write your AI policy, your oversight procedure, or your transparency notice. It checks that the controls behind them ran.

Not a risk register

Risk identification, scoring, and treatment stay in your existing register. The loop evidences controls, it doesn't rank risks.

Not an LLM-based compliance checker

Zero LLM in the evidence path. Every check is deterministic code. If it can't be replayed byte-identically, it isn't in the artifact.
